ABOUT ME

Engineer. Builder. Teammate.

Hi, I’m Zane.

I’m a computer engineering student at Georgia Tech, pursuing a minor in Computing and Intelligence. I enjoy projects that connect software with the physical world — one semester that’s a Django web app, the next it’s a corrector magnet mount for a particle accelerator.

What ties it together is wanting to understand a system end to end, not just get it working. My projects have taken me through programming, modeling, fabrication, and testing, and I’m happiest in the middle of a problem I haven’t solved yet.

Outside engineering, I play for Georgia Tech’s club ACHA Division II hockey team, and I bring several years of Science Olympiad leadership and STEM competition experience with me wherever I go next.
