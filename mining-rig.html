INDEPENDENT HARDWARE PROJECT / 2020–2025

GPU mining rig &
sustainability analysis.

700+MH/s

Historical mining performance

Performance with a practical trade-off.

I built an Ethereum mining rig and optimized the GPU configuration, cooling system, and power efficiency, achieving more than 700 MH/s.

The project extended beyond assembling hardware. I researched the environmental impacts of cryptocurrency mining, including energy consumption, cooling requirements, and the trade-offs between performance and sustainability.

I explored strategies to balance mining performance with energy efficiency, considering real-world power usage and carbon footprint.
